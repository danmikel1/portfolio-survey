# Client Presentation & Portfolio Workflow Survey

**Market research \| Estimated completion time: 5--7 minutes**

## Introduction

We're conducting research to understand how service professionals
prepare portfolios, project presentations, and proposals when pitching
to prospective clients.

This survey is for architects, engineers, interior designers,
contractors, consultants, agencies, and other professionals who sell
services to clients.

There are no right or wrong answers. We're interested in your actual
experiences and current workflow. Your responses will help us understand
where presentation and client-pitching processes can be improved.

Please answer based on your own experience. Questions marked "Select all
that apply" allow multiple answers.
